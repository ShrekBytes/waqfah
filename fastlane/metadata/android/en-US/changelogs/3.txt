First F-Droid release.

* Read a Quranic ayah before chosen apps open
* Sequential or random reading, with progress across the whole Quran
* Indopak and Uthmani scripts, several Arabic fonts, adjustable sizes
* Optional transliteration and English or Bengali translations, with more as downloads
* Per-app cooldown interval, or off
* English and Bengali interface, or follow the system language
* No accounts, no ads, no trackers; works offline apart from optional translation downloads

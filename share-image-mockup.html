Share image — final

Every decision from this session, drawn. Width 1080px; height follows the content; the palette and the content settings are the reader's.

  • Frame — 1dp line, inset uniformly 18px on all four sides, bottom rule interrupted behind the wordmark.
  • Ornament — accent four-point star on the divider above the aids, flanked by 44px hairlines, drawn as a path.
  • Content — padded 52px top and bottom, so it sits centred between the rules.
  • Wordmark — inkSoft at 55% alpha, centred on the bottom rule, letterspaced 2px.
  • Mirror — the active palette, and pronunciation and translation only if the reader has them on. A peeked translation is what gets shared.

2 · A long ayah

2:255. Same width, same frame, same ornament, same signature — the page simply gets taller. This is the case that justifies refusing a height cap, and it is also the check that the frame's rules stretch without the ornament drifting.
